## 1. Architectural Overview

Subphase 4.1.2 implements the complete ECMA-376 Part 1 DrawingML parametric geometry engine in `crates/ppt-engine/src/drawingml/`. It provides:
1. **Guide Formula Evaluator (`guide.rs`):**
   - Full evaluation of all 17 DrawingML arithmetic, geometric, and trigonometric operations (`val`, `+-`, `*/`, `+/`, `?:`, `abs`, `at2`, `cat2`, `sat2`, `cos`, `sin`, `tan`, `sqrt`, `min`, `max`, `pin`, `mod`).
   - Integrated `GuideContext` symbol table initialized with shape bounds (`w`, `h`, `l`, `t`, `r`, `b`, `hc`, `vc`, `ss`, `ls`, `ssd2`) and DrawingML angle constants (`cd2`, `cd4`, `cd8`, `3cd4`, `3cd8`, `5cd8`, `7cd8` in 60,000ths of a degree).
   - Sequential guide chaining where subsequent formulas can reference previously calculated guides.
   - 128-bit intermediate arithmetic to prevent integer overflow on large EMU coordinates.
2. **Normalized Vector Path Model (`path.rs`):**
   - Clean enum `PathCommand`: `MoveTo`, `LineTo`, `QuadTo`, `CubicTo`, `ArcTo`, `Close`.
   - Elliptical arc expansion (`to_cubic_beziers`): converts DrawingML elliptical arcs into sequences of $G^1$-continuous cubic Bezier curves ($\le 90^\circ$ span).
   - SVG path data serializer (`to_svg_path_data`) emitting compliant `d="..."` strings.
   - Axis-aligned bounding box calculation and even-odd ray-casting point-in-path hit testing (`contains_point`).
3. **187 Preset Geometries Catalog (`preset.rs`):**
   - Complete catalog of all 187 preset shapes defined in ECMA-376 `ST_ShapeType`:
     - Rectangles and clipped rectangles (10 shapes: `rect`, `roundRect`, `snip1Rect`, `plaque`, etc.)
     - Polygons (12 shapes: `triangle`, `rtTriangle`, `diamond`, `parallelogram`, `trapezoid`, `pentagon`..`dodecagon`)
     - Stars (10 shapes: `star4`..`star32`)
     - Block arrows (26 shapes: `rightArrow`, `leftArrow`, `upArrow`, `downArrow`, `leftRightArrow`, `chevron`, `notchedRightArrow`, etc.)
     - Curves & symbols (15 shapes: `ellipse`, `heart`, `lightningBolt`, `donut`, `pie`, `sun`, `moon`, `cloud`, etc.)
     - Flowchart symbols (29 shapes: `flowChartProcess`, `flowChartDecision`, `flowChartTerminator`, etc.)
     - Action buttons (12 shapes: `actionButtonBlank` through `actionButtonMovie`)
     - Math symbols (6 shapes: `mathPlus`, `mathMinus`, `mathMultiply`, `mathDivide`, `mathEqual`, `mathNotEqual`)
     - Lines, connectors, brackets, and braces (17 shapes)
     - Banners, scrolls, waves (8 shapes)
     - 3D shapes, frames, and miscellaneous (22 shapes)
4. **Geometry Compiler (`compiler.rs`):**
   - `GeometryCompiler::compile_preset`: compiles standard preset geometries with custom adjust values (`a:avLst`).
   - `GeometryCompiler::compile_custom`: compiles custom geometries (`a:custGeom`) with custom guides, adjust values, and path lists.
   - `ResolvedShape::evaluate_paths`: seamless integration with the 3-tier cascade solver.
