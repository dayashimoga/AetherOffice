## 1. Architectural Overview

Subphase 4.2.1 implements a 120Hz frame-accurate SMIL animation state machine and timeline engine in `crates/ppt-engine/src/animation/`:
1. **SMIL Timing DOM (`model.rs`):**
   - Full typed data structures for PresentationML animation: `SlideTiming`, `TimeNode`, `CommonTimeNode`, `TimeNodeKind`, `TriggerType`, `TimeNodeFill`, and `TimeNodeRestart`.
   - Node variants: `Parallel` (`p:par`), `Sequential` (`p:seq`), `Motion` (`p:animMotion`), `Rotation` (`p:animRot`), `Scale` (`p:animScale`), `Color` (`p:animClr`), `Generic` (`p:anim`), `Effect` (`p:animEffect`), and `Set` (`p:set`).
2. **Easing & Timing Dynamics (`easing.rs`):**
   - Implements ECMA-376 Section 19.5 piecewise acceleration/deceleration S-curve easing:
     - $v_{\max} = \frac{2}{2 - a - d}$
     - Acceleration phase: $p(t) = \frac{v_{\max}}{2a} t^2$
     - Constant velocity phase: $p(t) = \frac{a \cdot v_{\max}}{2} + v_{\max} (t - a)$
     - Deceleration phase: $p(t) = 1 - \frac{v_{\max}}{2d} (1 - t)^2$
   - General cubic Bezier easing solver for `keySplines`.
   - Repetition count (finite and indefinite) and auto-reverse (ping-pong trajectory).
3. **Property Interpolators (`interpolator.rs`):**
   - Arc-length normalized multi-segment motion path traversal (`interpolate_motion`).
   - Rotation interpolation across relative and absolute angular spans (`interpolate_rotation`).
   - 2D uniform and non-uniform scale interpolation (`interpolate_scale`).
   - Dual color-space interpolation: RGB linear and HSL hue-wheel traversal with direction control (`interpolate_color`).
   - Entrance and exit transition effects with clip progress and opacity (`interpolate_effect`).
4. **120Hz Deterministic Timeline & Click State Machine (`timeline.rs`):**
   - `AnimationTimeline` organizes animations into interactive click steps (Step 0: on slide load; Steps 1..N: user click triggers).
   - Frame-accurate 120Hz sampling: `sample_at(time_ms)` and `sample_frame(frame_index, 120)` with 8.333ms frame budget.
   - Interactive click navigation: `next_click()`, `prev_click()`, `seek_click(step)`.
   - Multi-shape scene state accumulation (`AnimatedSceneState`, `AnimatedShapeState`).
5. **Streaming Parser & Serializer:**
   - Full integration with PresentationML `Slide` (`p:sld/p:timing`).
